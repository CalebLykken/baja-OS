Sources for the traffic light, stop and yield icons on the map
(tools/make_sign_icons.py turns them into web/map/icons/*.png):

- stop-R1-1.svg, yield-R1-2.svg: the US STOP (R1-1) and YIELD (R1-2) signs
  from the Federal Highway Administration's Manual on Uniform Traffic
  Control Devices, via Wikimedia Commons. US government work: public domain.
- traffic-light-noto.png: the "vertical traffic light" emoji (U+1F6A6) from
  Google's Noto Emoji, 3D style, https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji,
  trimmed. Copyright Google LLC; see LICENSE-noto-emoji.txt (the project's
  README puts its images under the Apache License 2.0, LICENSE-apache-2.0.txt).
